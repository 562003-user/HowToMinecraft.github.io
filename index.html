What to do in the overworld

  1. Find the nearest tree, punch it to collect wood
  2. Use wood to make wood planks and make a crafting table
  3. Use the crafting table to make tools and other items
  4. Obtain a bed to make nights pass by faster and set a spawn point
  5. With your tools, go mining to get better tools and armor
  6. Next, you want to get obsidian and flint and steel

Tips for Overworld

  • To get obsidian, you need to find lava and pour it onto the lava source to make obsidian
  • Keep in mind you need a diamond or netherite pickaxe to mine the obsidian, and an iron pickaxe is needed to mine the ores for it

How to get to and what to do in the Nether

  1. to get to the Nether, you can either find a ruined portal or collect the obsidian and place it down yourself, then ignite it with flint and steel
  2. next after igniting the Nether portal with the flint and steel, enter the portal, and you will be teleported into the nether
  3. After entering the Nether, you will want to find a Nether fortress and enter it
  4. once you get into the Nether fortress, you want to locate the blaze spawner
  5. Kill the blazes once you have located it, and they will drop blaze rods, which can be crafted into blaze powder

Tips for Nether

  • When you are in the Nether, you can mine for ancient debris(only with diamond or neitherite picaxes), which can be used to make neitherite armor and tools
  • What is recommended is wearing a piece of gold armor to keep piglins from attacking you

Entering the End dimension

  1. To enter the End, you first need to get Ender Pearls. You can get them by killing Endermen or trading with Piglins or villgers
  2. Next, with the Ender Pearls you have, you can craft an Eye of Ender with your Blaze Powder and ender pearls
  3. Once you have the Eye of Ender, you can throw it a it will drop down once it is on top of the stronghold
  4. dig straight down once it lands on the ground
  5. next in the fortress, locate the end portal
  6. After finding the end portal, make sure you have 12 Eyes of Ender to place on the edges of the portal
  7. once you place the Eyes of Ender, go into the portal, and you will teleport to the End dimension

Tip for entering the End

  • When at the stronghold, watch out for mobs
  • Make sure when placing an Eye of Ender it is facing the portal not sideways
  • To get rid of mobs, you can break their spawner so they do not attack you or place

End dimension and defeating the Ender Dragon

  1. The first step in defeating it is making your way to the main island
  2. Next, there will be Endermen; if you do not look them in the eye, they will not attack
  3. you will see multiple towers with end crystals; you want to destroy all of them to prevent the end dragonn fromm regenerating
  4. After destroying all end crystals, you can now attack the end dragon without it regenerating
  5. Once killed, it will ignite a portal in the middle of the map, and an Ender Egg will drop
  6. after defeating it, jump into the void-looking portal in the middle, and it will play the cutscene before returning you ot your spawn point

Tips for the End and defeating ender dragon

  • when trying to destroy end crystals, shooting them with a bow is faster then building up and destroying them
  • placeing a bed End will explode, but bringing it to damage the Ender Dragon can defeat it faster
  • trying the break the end egg it wil teleport
  • To get the end egg, if you dig and place a torch underneath, then breaking the block the end egg is on will drop it
  • After defeating the Ender Dragon, an end gateway portal will spawn near the edge of the map throwing a ender peral in it will teleport you to the outer end island. This can be used to get the elytra for flying
